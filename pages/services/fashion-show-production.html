Plan your show

Richard Moody began as a runway model and moved into producing decades ago. That view from the runway shows in the shows he builds: seamless organization, considered set design and a spotlight on innovative fashion. Many of his productions weave in music, art and theater, so each element supports the others.

Based in Minneapolis and working across the Twin Cities, IAmMoody has produced shows for boutiques, designers, nonprofits and brands, from intimate trunk shows to couture galas.

What's included

Everything a runway needs.

  • Show concept, theme and run-of-show
  • Model casting and coordination
  • Runway, staging, lighting and set design
  • Music, performers and emcee
  • Backstage management and stylist coordination
  • Venue, guest experience, signage and clean-up
  • Publicity and community outreach to build attendance
  • Sponsor and charity partner coordination
