About

Style with purpose.

IAmMoody is a Minneapolis fashion show and event production company built around one person's energy and a community that shows up.

Founder & visionary

Richard Moody

Richard's path started on the runway as a model and grew into producing, choreographing and hosting events across Minneapolis, Paris and London. He has been a brand ambassador, an emcee and an unofficial spokesperson for the causes and companies he believes in.

Through creativity and impeccable taste, IAmMoody has become a trusted event team in the Twin Cities: original party concepts, runway shows with meticulous planning, and results full of color, texture and imagination.
