How it works

  • Nothing is deleted. Marking a post “Archive” only records your decision. The full export stays in iammoody-export/.
  • Shortcuts in the preview panel: ←/→ move between items, A/M (posts) or U/D (media) set the decision, Esc closes.
  • Bulk actions: filter the table, then set every filtered row at once (e.g. archive everything from 2009–2012).
  • Export: “Export CSV” downloads the filtered rows with your decisions and notes.
